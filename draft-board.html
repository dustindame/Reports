Help & FAQ
If this league is Pro, do I need Pro on this device too?
No — Pro belongs to the league, not any one device. Once a league is upgraded to Pro, every Pro feature (Break Screen, Recap, Fan Messages, and more) shows up here for anyone viewing it, regardless of which device or app is doing the viewing.
What does the Switch League button do?
Lets this device point at a different league without clearing its remembered code by hand — enter a different league's code and it switches over immediately.
Can more than one person log picks?
Yes — anyone with the league code and PIN can log picks from Bid Board on their own phone, even at the same time as someone else. The same player can never be drafted twice, even if two people enter it around the same moment.
What does the download icon do?
Downloads a CSV backup of every pick made so far, straight from this device — a safety net independent of anything else. (Not available inside the Fire TV app itself, since there's nowhere on a TV to save or open a downloaded file.)
How do people follow along on their phone?
Scan the QR code — it opens Team Picks with this league already loaded, no code needed.
Don't have a Fire TV or Android TV? You can still show this on a big screen.
This screen is just a regular webpage — no app is required. Open a browser on anything you can get onto the TV (a laptop over HDMI, a smart TV's built-in browser, a Chromecast, casting a phone tab) and go to reports.bidboard.workers.dev/watch.html — type in your league code there and it takes you straight to this screen.
Will this league ever get deleted?
Free-tier leagues are cleared automatically after 7 idle days if unfinished, or 14 idle days once complete. Pro leagues last much longer — 60 idle days — since a draft doesn't need to stick around forever once it's been copied into your actual fantasy service. Everything goes, including the Recap page and its stats/roast — download Recap first (from a phone or desktop, not this TV app) if you want to keep it.
