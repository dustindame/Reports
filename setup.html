What's in Pro PRO

Everything above marked PRO is part of a one-time upgrade. Free already covers a full draft — up to 14 teams, every position, up to 2 saved leagues, board snapshots, the news ticker, elapsed time, and a custom board name.

  • Recap Page — full post-draft stats: price trends, team composition, spend by position, and (if turned on) the roast section.
  • Break Screen — the Draft Board switches to trivia, season odds, and all-time stat leaders whenever the draft is on break.
  • Recent Picks & Drafted by Position — two more live stat widgets on the Draft Board header.
  • Fun Extras — NICE celebrations, Fan Messages, Shots, and the Recap roast section.
  • CSV Export — download the full draft as a spreadsheet from Team Picks.
  • More saved leagues — beyond 2 saved leagues per device.

Pro leagues stick around for 60 idle days (free leagues: 7–14) — plenty of time to finish a draft, but not kept forever. When that window closes, everything is deleted, including the Recap page and its stats/roast — download it first if you want to keep it.

One purchase unlocks Pro on this account going forward — every league you create afterward starts as Pro automatically, no need to buy again. Any league you upgrade to Pro shows Pro everywhere it's viewed, on any device or app.
