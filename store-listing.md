# Store Listing Copy

Draft copy for Google Play (phone app) and the Amazon Appstore (Fire TV app). Review and adjust before submitting — this is a starting point, not final marketing copy set in stone.

---

## Google Play — Draft Entry (phone app)

**Title** (max 30 characters): `Draft Entry — Auction Draft`

**Short description** (max 80 characters):
`Log fantasy football auction picks live, synced to your TV in real time.`

**Full description** (max 4000 characters):

> Run your fantasy football auction draft like a pro — no spreadsheet, no yelling across the room.
>
> Draft Entry is the phone-side companion to Draft Board (the TV display): search a player, tap the winning team, slide to the winning bid, and confirm. Every pick shows up instantly on your TV and on everyone else's phones — no refreshing, no "wait, did that save?"
>
> **Built for how auction drafts actually work:**
> - No fixed turn order — any team can bid on any player, whenever it happens
> - Multiple people can log picks at once (handy for splitting up duty, or handing off if someone has to step away)
> - The same player can never be drafted twice, even if two people enter it around the same moment
> - Undo any pick instantly if a mistake gets logged
>
> **Set up your league in minutes:**
> - 6–14 teams, custom budget, custom roster positions (including Kicker and Superflex)
> - A shareable league code and commissioner PIN — viewing is open, editing is protected
> - A free tier that covers a full draft: up to 10 teams, every position, board snapshots, and more
>
> **Pro unlocks:**
> - A full post-draft Recap page with price trends, spend breakdowns, and (if you want it) a savage AI-generated roast of every team's draft
> - A Break Screen with trivia, season odds, and all-time stat leaders for when the draft pauses
> - Fan Messages, celebratory NICE moments, and Shots — small touches that make a draft night feel like an event
> - Pro belongs to your whole league, not just your phone — anyone with your league's code can use every Pro feature once it's unlocked, no extra purchase needed
>
> Pair it with the free Draft Board app for Fire TV / Android TV to put the whole league up on the big screen.

**Category:** Sports

## Amazon Appstore — Draft Board (Fire TV / Android TV app)

**Title** (max ~50 characters): `Draft Board — Fantasy Auction Display`

**Short description:**
`A live, auto-updating draft board for your fantasy football auction — every roster, every budget, on the big screen.`

**Full description:**

> Turn any TV into a live fantasy football auction draft board.
>
> Draft Board shows every team's roster, remaining budget, and recent picks in one wide display that updates instantly as picks are logged from the free Draft Entry phone app. No manual refreshing, no spreadsheet on a laptop propped up in the corner.
>
> **What's on screen:**
> - Every team's full roster and budget, color-coded by position
> - A live scrolling NFL news ticker
> - A scannable QR code so anyone can check any team's roster on their own phone
> - A real-time clock and draft progress tracker
>
> **Pro leagues also get:**
> - A Break Screen with trivia, season odds, and all-time stat leaders for pauses in the draft
> - Fan Messages from the room, shown right on the ticker
> - A full post-draft Recap page, one tap away
>
> This app is a passive display — pair it with the free Draft Entry phone app (available on Google Play) to actually run your draft. No purchase needed on this app itself; league features are unlocked through Draft Entry.
>
> Not affiliated with or endorsed by the NFL or any NFL team.

**Category:** Sports
