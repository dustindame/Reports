What this is

A tool for running a live fantasy football auction draft: a phone screen for logging picks, a TV-facing display for following along, and a lookup page for checking a team's roster. It is an independent, unofficial tool — it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the NFL, any NFL team, or any real fantasy sports platform. References to real players, teams, and public NFL facts/statistics are used in the same way any fantasy sports commentary or tool does — as public factual information, not as a claim of official partnership.

Accounts & access

Running a league itself needs no account. A league is identified by a shareable code, and edited using a commissioner-chosen PIN. You are responsible for keeping your PIN and league code appropriately private within your own group — we cannot recover a lost PIN, and we cannot restore a league once its automatic retention period has passed (see the Privacy Policy for exact timing). Signing in with Google is only ever needed to buy or restore the optional Pro upgrade.

Free tier & Pro upgrade

A free tier is available with limits on team count and the number of saved leagues per device. A one-time Pro upgrade removes those limits and unlocks additional display features. Pro status belongs to the league itself, not to any single device — once a league is upgraded, anyone with its code and PIN gets full access to its Pro features.
